## Module 1: Monthly Roadmap & Deliverable Framework

### Step 1 — Understand Monthly Commitments
Before building the roadmap, confirm the following with the client or from the onboarding brief:
- How many posts per platform per month?
- What content types are committed? (static graphics, reels, carousels, stories, long-form videos, shorts)
- Which platforms are in scope?
- Are there any product launches, events, or promotions this month?
- What is the content approval SLA? (e.g., 3 business days for client review)

### Step 2 — Build the Monthly Content Calendar
Structure the calendar using this framework:

| Week | Content Pillar | Format | Platform(s) | SEO Angle | Status |
|------|---------------|--------|-------------|-----------|--------|
| W1   | Educational   | Carousel | Instagram, LinkedIn | Target keyword: [keyword] | Draft |
| W2   | Brand Story   | Reel/Short | Instagram, YouTube | Search intent: [intent] | Draft |
| W3   | Social Proof  | Graphic | Facebook, LinkedIn | Addresses query: [query] | Draft |
| W4   | Engagement    | Poll/Story | Instagram, Facebook | Topical cluster support | Draft |

**The Four Content Pillars for SRS Clients:**
1. **Educational** — How-to, tips, explainers. Highest SEO value.
2. **Brand Story** — Who the client is, their values, behind the scenes.
3. **Social Proof** — Testimonials, case studies, results, reviews.
4. **Engagement** — Questions, polls, CTAs, community building.

Maintain a **70/20/10 ratio**:
- 70% Educational + Social Proof (search-informed, value-driven)
- 20% Brand Story (brand awareness)
- 10% Promotional (direct offers)

## Module 3: Content Creation — Copywriting, Hashtags & Research

### Step 1 — Deep Research Before Writing
For every post, research:
- What questions people are searching related to this topic (use keyword/question angles)
- What top-performing posts in the niche look like for this format
- Trending audio, formats, or hooks for Reels/Shorts (if applicable)
- Relevant statistics, quotes, or data points to add credibility

### Step 2 — Copywriting Framework

Every post must follow the **Hook → Value → CTA** structure:

```
HOOK (Line 1 — stop the scroll):
  - Start with a question, surprising stat, bold statement, or relatable pain point
  - Never start with the brand name

VALUE (Body — deliver the promise):
  - Educate, entertain, or inspire
  - Keep sentences short. Use line breaks liberally.
  - Speak TO the audience, not AT them

CTA (Final line — drive action):
  - "Save this for later", "Comment below", "Link in bio", "Share this with..."
  - One clear CTA per post. Never two.
```

**Tone Calibration by Platform:**

| Platform | Tone | Copy Length | Emoji Use |
|----------|------|------------|-----------|
| Instagram | Conversational, aspirational | Medium (150-300 chars above fold) | Moderate |
| Facebook | Informative, community | Long-form OK | Low-Moderate |
| LinkedIn | Professional, authoritative | Long-form preferred | Minimal |
| Twitter/X | Punchy, opinionated | Under 280 chars | Low |
| YouTube | Descriptive, keyword-rich | Long (SEO optimized description) | None in title |
| Pinterest | Inspirational, keyword-heavy | Short caption, keyword in title | None |

### Step 3 — Hashtag Research Strategy

Use a **3-Tier Hashtag Stack** for Instagram and Facebook:

- **Tier 1 — Niche Hashtags** (5–10 tags): 10K–200K posts. Most targeted. Highest chance of ranking.
- **Tier 2 — Industry Hashtags** (5–8 tags): 200K–1M posts. Broader reach.
- **Tier 3 — Trending/Brand Hashtags** (2–5 tags): 1M+ posts or brand-specific. Visibility play.

**Rules:**
- Never use banned or spammy hashtags
- Rotate hashtag sets — don't use the same set every post
- For LinkedIn: 3–5 hashtags max, highly relevant only
- For YouTube: use tags field + keywords in title and description
- For Pinterest: use keywords in the pin title and description (Pinterest is a search engine)
- For Twitter/X: 1–2 hashtags maximum

### Step 4 — SEO Integration in Copy

Every piece of content must embed SEO thinking:
- Use the **target keyword or its semantic variation** naturally in the caption
- Frame educational posts as answers to real search queries ("Here's how to [keyword topic]...")
- For YouTube: title must include the primary keyword. First 2 lines of description must include primary + secondary keyword. Use chapters/timestamps.
- For Pinterest: treat every pin description like a mini blog meta description — keyword-rich, informative, 100–200 words.
- For LinkedIn articles: full SEO article treatment — H2s, keyword density, internal links to client site.

## Module 4: Cross-Platform Publishing Guidelines

### Platform-Specific Specifications

**Instagram**
- Feed: 1080x1080 (square) or 1080x1350 (portrait)
- Reels: 1080x1920 (9:16), max 90 seconds for best reach
- Stories: 1080x1920, use interactive stickers (polls, questions)
- Best posting times: Tue–Fri, 9AM–11AM or 6PM–8PM (local time)
- Frequency: 4–7 posts/week (feed + reels combined)

**Facebook**
- Feed image: 1200x630
- Reels: Same as Instagram
- Best times: Wed–Fri, 1PM–4PM
- Frequency: 3–5 posts/week
- Always include a link where relevant (Facebook's algorithm deprioritizes external links in some formats — use first comment strategy)

**LinkedIn**
- Image: 1200x627
- Video: 1920x1080 (landscape), max 10 min
- Best times: Tue–Thu, 8AM–10AM or 5PM–6PM
- Frequency: 3–5 posts/week
- Engage with comments within the first hour of posting (critical for LinkedIn algorithm)

**YouTube**
- Thumbnail: 1280x720 (max 2MB)
- Title: 60 characters max, keyword at the front
- Description: First 200 chars visible without expanding — make them count
- Tags: 10–15 relevant tags including exact-match and broad keywords
- Shorts: 1080x1920, under 60 seconds, must loop well
- Frequency: 1–2 long-form videos/week + 3–5 Shorts

**Pinterest**
- Pin size: 1000x1500 (2:3 ratio)
- Title: 100 chars, keyword-first
- Description: 200–500 chars, keyword-rich, natural language
- Board organization: Create topical boards aligned with client's keyword clusters
- Frequency: 5–15 pins/day (including repins). Schedule using Tailwind or native scheduler.
- Pinterest is a **search engine** — treat every pin like an SEO asset.

**Twitter/X**
- Image: 1200x675 (16:9)
- Best times: Mon–Thu, 9AM–12PM
- Frequency: 3–5 tweets/day (including replies and engagement)
- Use threads for educational deep-dives (high reach potential)

### Publishing Checklist (Before Every Post)

```
✅ Visual/creative reviewed and approved by client
✅ Caption proofread (spelling, grammar, tone)
✅ CTA is clear and singular
✅ Hashtags researched and verified (no banned tags)
✅ SEO keyword naturally embedded
✅ Links tracked with UTM parameters (if applicable)
✅ Post scheduled at optimal time for the platform
✅ Alt-text added to images (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook)
✅ First comment prepared with hashtags (Instagram strategy)
✅ Cross-posting adaptations made (don't copy-paste same caption)
```

This is what separates SRS from a generic social media agency. Every social media decision must be informed by the SEO roadmap.

### The SEO-Social Bridge Checklist

- [ ] Are this month's social topics aligned with the **keyword clusters** from the SEO roadmap?
- [ ] Are long-form blog posts being **repurposed** into carousels, reels, and infographics?
- [ ] Are social captions using **semantic keyword variations** (not keyword stuffing)?

- [ ] Are social posts driving **traffic back to SEO-optimized landing pages or blog posts**?
- [ ] Are social profiles properly **interlinked** and consistent with NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data from local SEO efforts?

### Content Repurposing Workflow

```
1 Long-Form Blog Post → Can become:
  ├── 1 LinkedIn article (condensed, first-person)
  ├── 1 Instagram carousel (5–10 slides, key takeaways)
  ├── 3–5 Twitter/X posts (individual tips from the post)
  ├── 1 YouTube video or Short (explainer version)
  ├── 2–3 Pinterest pins (different angles, same topic)
  └── 1 Facebook post (summary + link to full post)
```

Always attribute content clusters to their **parent keyword** and ensure social posts link back to the ranking page where relevant.

## Module 7: Platform Algorithm Updates (2025-2026)

Social media algorithms have shifted significantly. Here are the critical changes every SRS strategist must know.

### Instagram Algorithm Changes (2025-2026)

Instagram no longer uses a single algorithm — it runs **multiple AI-powered ranking systems** for Feed, Reels, Stories, and Explore, each with unique priorities.

**Top 3 Ranking Factors (Confirmed):**

1. **Watch Time** — how long users spend viewing your content
2. **DM Shares** — weighted **3-5x higher than likes**. Content shared privately is the strongest signal
3. **Likes per Reach** — engagement rate relative to how many people saw it

**Key Changes to Strategy:**

| Change | Impact on SRS Strategy |
|--------|----------------------|
| Original content rewarded (40-60% more reach) | Never repost or aggregate other creators' content |
| Aggregator accounts crushed (60-80% reach drop) | All content must be original to the client |
| Hashtags have reduced algorithmic weight | Shift budget from hashtag research to **keyword-based SEO** in captions, bio, and alt text |
| Topic clarity from last 9-12 posts | Maintain **niche consistency** — random off-topic posts trigger distribution penalties |
| First 2-3 seconds of Reels decide fate | Hook MUST land in 2 seconds. No slow intros, no logo animation openers |
| Stories posting multiple per day is fine | No reach penalty for multiple stories per day |
| Reply to comments within 30 minutes | Set a team SOP for rapid comment engagement |

**Instagram SEO (Replaces Hashtag Strategy):**
- Optimise bio with primary keywords
- Use keywords naturally in captions (first 2 lines)
- Add descriptive alt text to every image
- Use location tags strategically
- Caption keywords are now more important than hashtags for discovery

### LinkedIn Algorithm Changes (2025-2026)

LinkedIn has moved from rewarding virality to rewarding **relevance** and **expertise**.

**Key Changes:**

| Change | Impact on SRS Strategy |
|--------|----------------------|
| Relevance over recency — older relevant posts can appear at top | Write evergreen expert content, not just timely takes |
| Expert and knowledge-driven content prioritised | Position clients as niche authorities, not generalists |
| Meaningful comments are "gold" — generic "I agree!" is worthless | Train clients to write thoughtful responses on industry posts |
| Dwell time is a ranking factor | Create content that holds attention — stories, data, insights |
| Profile views after a post signal quality | Ensure client profiles are fully optimised with keywords |
| 1st-degree connections prioritised | Quality of network matters more than size |
| Video and carousels prioritised over text-only posts | Include visual elements in every post |
| External links no longer penalised in body text | Can safely include links inline (no more "link in comments" dance) |
| Engagement bait detected and deprioritised | Never use "Comment YES if you agree" type posts |
| AI uses LLMs to understand post context | Write clearly and specifically — vague posts get less distribution |

**LinkedIn Best Practices for SRS Clients:**
- Post 3-5x per week, consistently at the same times
- Use document carousels (PDF uploads) for highest engagement
- Write in first person with real opinions and experiences
- Tag people sparingly and only when genuinely relevant
- Video is being boosted again in 2026 — prioritise short vertical videos

### Emerging Platforms

**Threads (Meta):**
- Growing as a text-first platform alongside Instagram
- Cross-posting from Instagram now supported
- Best for: real-time engagement, industry commentary, building community
- Strategy: Use it for quick takes and commentary that would not suit Instagram's visual-first format
- Ideal posting frequency: 2-3 posts/day

**WhatsApp Channels:**
- One-to-many broadcast feature — like a newsletter inside WhatsApp
- Best for: Indian audiences (WhatsApp is the dominant messaging app)
- Strategy: Share blog post summaries, quick tips, event announcements
- Drive users from social profiles to subscribe to the channel
- Ideal frequency: 2-3 updates/week (too frequent = muted)

**Social Commerce (In-App Shopping):**
- Instagram Checkout and TikTok Shop are expanding
- For e-commerce clients: tag products directly in posts and reels
- Create "shoppable" content that demonstrates products in use
- Video product demonstrations outperform static product shots

## SRS Social Media Principles — Never Violate

1. **Social is an amplifier of search, never a standalone silo.** Every post supports the SEO roadmap.
2. **Original content only.** Never repost, aggregate, or recycle other creators' work without transformation. Algorithms now detect and penalise this.
3. **Keywords over hashtags (Instagram 2025+).** Invest in caption SEO, not hashtag stacks.
4. **Quality over quantity.** One excellent post beats three mediocre ones. Always.
5. **Respond within 30 minutes.** Comment engagement speed directly affects reach.
6. **Data first, opinions second.** Frame all client reports around measurable business outcomes.
7. **Consult the `copywriting-seo-srs` skill** for all written content — captions, carousel text, video scripts.
8. **Mobile preview every post.** If it does not look good at 375px width, do not publish it.
9. **Never go dark.** Clients should hear from SRS at least weekly, regardless of posting schedule.
10. **Test, measure, iterate.** What worked last month may not work this month. Algorithms evolve. So do we.
