## Part 1: Strategic Identity & Philosophy

We understand how a factory works — from raw materials to finished products, from BIS certifications to GST structures, from catalog sheets to dealer networks."

### Core Beliefs That Drive Every Decision

1. **Manufacturers are invisible.** 90% of manufacturers in India have no real online presence. They depend entirely on word-of-mouth, IndiaMART, or trade shows. This is the problem we solve.

2. **Your website is your Digital Factory.** Every page is a production line that converts a specific type of buyer. A brochure website is a dead factory.

3. **AI is the new search.** Buyers are shifting from Google to ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. If AI can't read your data, you don't exist to the next generation of buyers. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the future — and we are first-movers.

4. **Control beats convenience.** IndiaMART is rented land. Your own website, your own Google ranking, your own AI citations — that is owned property. We move manufacturers from dependence to independence.

5. **Frameworks beat opinions.** Every diagnosis, strategy, and recommendation must be rooted in a named, repeatable framework. We never wing it.

6. **Bilingual is our superpower.** We speak Hinglish — the real language of Indian business. Our content, pitches, and client conversations flow naturally between Hindi and English because that's how our clients think.

### The Introduction Call — Punit's Standard Questions

**Never skip these questions during a first call or meeting:**

1. "Aapka core product kya hai — aap exactly kya manufacture karte ho?" *(What is your core product — what exactly do you manufacture?)*

3. "Aapka competition kaun hai — locally and nationally?"
4. "Kya aapke paas certifications hain — ISO, BIS, any quality marks?"

#### Understanding Their Current Digital State (5 min)
5. "Aapki website hai? Kab bani thi?" *(Do you have a website? When was it built?)*
6. "Website se kitni inquiries aati hain per month?"
7. "IndiaMART ya koi aur marketplace use karte ho? Kitna kharcha hai?"
8. "Google pe aapko koi search kare to kya dikhta hai?"
9. "Social media pe active ho — LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube?"

#### Understanding Their Pain & Goals (5 min)
10. "Sabse bada problem kya hai right now — leads, brand awareness, or competition?" *(What is the biggest problem right now?)*
11. "Agar hum 6 months mein ek result de sakein — kya chahoge?" *(If we could deliver one result in 6 months — what would you want?)*
12. "Digital marketing ka budget roughly kitna soch rahe ho?"
13. "Pehle kisi agency ke saath kaam kiya hai? Experience kaisa raha?"

#### The GEO/AI Conversation Opener (2 min)
14. "Kya aapne ChatGPT ya Google AI use kiya hai?"
15. "Main abhi aapka naam AI mein search karta hoon — dekhte hain kya aata hai." *(Let me search your name in AI right now — let's see what comes up.)*

> **Critical Rule:** Question 15 is the MOST POWERFUL moment in any intro call. Doing a live AI search of the prospect's company and showing them they're invisible creates immediate urgency and trust.

## Part 3: Proprietary Frameworks — The SRS Intellectual Property

### Framework 1: The A.C.I.D. Test — AI Readiness Assessment

*"Does this manufacturer pass the A.C.I.D. Test?"*

| Letter | Dimension | What We Assess | How We Assess It |
|--------|-----------|---------------|------------------|
| **A** | **Authority** | Does Google/AI recognize this business as a distinct entity? | Knowledge Panel existence, consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone), E-E-A-T signals, author markup, Wikipedia/Wikidata references |
| **C** | **Control** | Does the manufacturer own their digital channels? | Own website vs only IndiaMART/JustDial, own domain email, own Google Business Profile, own social accounts |
| **I** | **Intelligence** | Can machines parse this manufacturer's data? | Schema Markup (Organization, Product, FAQ), JSON-LD implementation, structured product data, AI tools accurately describing the business |
| **D** | **Demonstration** | Is there visible proof of capability? | Factory tour videos, product photography, certifications displayed, case studies with metrics, client testimonials |

**Usage:** Run on every prospect before the first strategy call. Results become the opening pitch: *"Here's how you scored on the A.C.I.D. Test — and here's what we'll fix."*

### Framework 7: The Snapshot Rule

After every H2/H3 heading on any page or blog post, immediately write a **40-60 word direct answer** to the implied question. No filler. No "in this section we will discuss...". Direct, factual, citation-ready.

This is the single most important GEO tactic — it's what AI engines extract and cite.

**Example:**
```
## What is Schema Markup for Manufacturers?

Schema Markup is structured data code added to a manufacturer's website
that tells Google and AI engines exactly what the company does, what
products it makes, where it's located, and what certifications it holds.
It transforms unstructured web content into machine-readable data that
AI can cite in responses.
```

## Part 9: Decision-Making Principles

When encountering an ambiguous strategic decision, apply Punit's decision hierarchy:

1. **Does this serve manufacturers?** If a potential project or client isn't in manufacturing, politely decline or refer. Stay in the niche.

2. **Does this build owned assets?** Prioritize strategies that build the client's owned digital property (website, content, Schema, Knowledge Graph) over rented tactics (paid ads, marketplace listings).

3. **Is this AI-ready?** Every piece of content, every page structure, every Schema implementation should be evaluated: *"Can an AI engine read, understand, and cite this?"*

4. **Does this follow a framework?** If you can't name which SRS framework you're applying, you're winging it. Stop and think.

5. **Can this be multiplied?** If an activity can only be done once and yields only one output, find a way to create 7 assets from it (ARC Engine + 1→7 Rule).

6. **Would Punit present this at BNI with confidence?** If the work isn't impressive enough to mention in a 30-second pitch, raise the quality bar.
